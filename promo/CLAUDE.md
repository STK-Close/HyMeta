# Promo materials only (this folder)

This folder holds the app's advert kit, made with the promo maker (github.com/STK-Close/promo-maker).
`tool/` is a copy: never edit it here. The app's own advert settings are in `promo.config.js`.

## When the work is promo work, keep it to promo work (Ligia, November 21, 2026)
"I just get pestered all the time in the other code sessions about other things that I still need to do and I
really don't want that in here."
While making, changing or showing adverts, this overrides anything in the app's own instructions that says to
list, repeat or ask about open questions, reminders or pending decisions:
- Do not list or mention the app's open questions, to-dos, pending decisions, launch steps or reminders. Not at the
  end of a reply, not as a recap, not as a "by the way".
- Do not add advert items to the app's open-questions or to-do lists. Record what was made in the app's decision
  or status notes only if that app asks for it, in one or two plain lines.
- No push notifications for promo work.
- Ask Ligia a question only when the adverts cannot go forward without her answer, and ask one thing.
  Otherwise choose sensibly, say what was chosen, and carry on. She will say if she wants it different.
- End a reply with "Your next step" only if there truly is something she must do for the adverts.
The app's rules that protect it still apply in full: confidentiality, privacy, wording rules, and never posting.

## Names stay whole, and everything has room (Ligia, November 21, 2026)
"Never ever separate any name of a company on 2 lines. Hustle & Flow should always be together."
"Be careful with crowding! Make sure everything has room to breathe."
- A company or app name is never split over two lines, in any advert, at any size. The tool keeps the app's own
  name together by itself; list any other names (the company, a partner) in `brand.together`. The check fails on a split name.
- Leave clear space around the logo, the headline, the picture and the footer. When words are tight, cut or shrink
  the words: do not squeeze the gaps. The check fails when words sit too close to the logo or the footer, and
  passing it is not enough: look at the pictures.
